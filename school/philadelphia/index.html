STUDENT EMAIL GUIDE

School District of Philadelphia

Find the documented student email format and official district resources for Philadelphia public schools.

Student email format

[8-digit student ID]@philasd.org

The documented format uses the student's eight-digit ID followed by @philasd.org. Use only the student ID assigned to you by the district.

How to find your student email

  1. Check your district-provided student account information.
  2. Use the official district website for account and school resources.
  3. If you do not know your student ID or cannot sign in, contact your school.
