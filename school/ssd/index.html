Scranton School District

Use the school-issued email address provided by your district when signing in to school services.

@ssdedu.org

For example, a district-issued address ends with @ssdedu.org. Your complete address must come from the school district.

Need help signing in?

  1. Use the school email address assigned to you by the district.
  2. If you are unsure of your full email address, check with your teacher or school IT team.
  3. Use the district's official website for school information and support.
