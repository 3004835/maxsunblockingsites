STUDENT EMAIL GUIDE

NYC Public Schools

Find the student email domain and official account instructions for New York City Public Schools.

Student email domain

@nycstudents.net

Student accounts use the @nycstudents.net domain. Your complete username is assigned by NYC Public Schools, so do not guess the part before the @ symbol.

How to find your student email

  1. Open the official NYC Public Schools account instructions.
  2. Follow the school's instructions to find your assigned account.
  3. If you cannot find your username, ask your school for help.
